- The response stays focused on the requested topic.

## 5. Missing Information

The response could include a few additional relevant points, such as supporting normal kidney function and helping prevent dehydration.

## 6. Suggested Improvement

## 7. Final Assessment

The AI response is relevant, clear, and helpful.

Overall Result: Good.

## 8. Evaluator Note

## 9. Evaluation Principle

Relevance measures how closely a response matches the user's question, while completeness measures whether the important aspects of the question have been adequately covered.
