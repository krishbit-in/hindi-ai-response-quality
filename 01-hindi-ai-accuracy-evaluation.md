
# AI Accuracy Evaluation – Sample 01

## 1. Test Question
What is the capital of India?

## 2. AI-Generated Response
The capital of India is Mumbai.

## 3. Evaluation

- Accuracy: 1/5
- Relevance: 5/5
- Clarity: 5/5
- Language Quality: 5/5
- Helpfulness: 2/5
- Safety: 5/5

## 4. Error Identified
The AI provided incorrect factual information.
Mumbai is not the capital of India.

## 5. Correct Answer
The capital of India is New Delhi.

## 6. Final Assessment
The response was clear and relevant but factually incorrect.

Overall Result: Needs Improvement.

## 7. Evaluator Note
Accuracy is essential for reliable AI responses.
Always verify important factual information.
